🎓 What Makes This Representation Unique? (Defense Theory)
Donald Knuth & Lester Ford's Original Insight (1959):
Sorting is fundamentally the conversion of a Partially Ordered Set (Poset) with 0 known relations into a Total Order (Chain). Standard sorting algorithms (like Bubble or QuickSort) waste comparisons on elements whose relative order is already partially implied. Ford-Johnson is the theoretical minimum-comparison sort because it structures comparisons as a balanced tournament tree of pairs, where losers hang from their winners. Inserting losers in reverse Jacobsthal order maximizes the information entropy gained at every single binary search step!
